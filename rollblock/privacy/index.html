Tipstone Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Tipstone ("the app") is made by Ola Lindsten ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We do not run any server and do not receive it.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, within the choices you make in the consent and tracking prompts.
  • In-app purchases go through Apple or Google and are managed by RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report so we can fix the bug. We do not use analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your progress (completed levels and best moves), your lives and when the next one arrives, your settings, how many ads you have seen (to space them out and to limit rewarded ads to five a day) and whether you bought "Remove ads". This information stays on your device; we do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can reset your progress in the app under Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that give you an extra life. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and limited to content suitable for all ages. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers: the Advertising ID on Android, and on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking (see below).
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped.
  • Diagnostic data about the SDK's performance.

Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices in the consent prompt and, on iOS, the tracking prompt. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use contextual information rather than your profile.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you buy "Remove ads", the app stops showing full-screen ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay available as an optional way to get an extra life.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Before any ad is loaded, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Your choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. Where it applies, you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices. If the app cannot check your consent status, for example because the consent service cannot be reached, it requests no ads until it can.

Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. This permission only affects whether Google can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads and measurement. The app works the same whether you allow it or not. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Notifications

The app can tell you when your lives are full again. It asks for permission only after you first run out of lives, never when you first open the app. These are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device, and no push service, server or device token is involved. You can turn them off in your device's notification settings.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google, receives a crash report: the error and where in the app it happened, the app version, device model and operating system, and a random installation ID used to count how many installations a crash affects. Crash reports contain no personal information and are used only to find and fix bugs.

In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

The app offers "Remove ads", a one-time purchase. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment card or billing details.

Purchases are managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which helps the app verify and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history for this app (products, prices, transaction identifiers and dates), and basic device information such as operating system, app version, country and language. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, push notifications or any server of our own.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact us at tipstone.app@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google and RevenueCat keep the information they receive according to their own policies; crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase data linked to your anonymous app user ID, contact us and we will forward the request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Since we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled by the consent and tracking settings described above, or by contacting Google or RevenueCat directly. You can also contact us and we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Sweden the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, https://www.imy.se/en), elsewhere the authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
