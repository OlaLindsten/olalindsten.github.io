Twinswipe Support

Questions, a level that looks wrong or a purchase that did not arrive: write to us and we will answer.

Common questions

I bought Remove ads but still see ads. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Remove ads takes away the ads between levels; videos you choose to watch for coins stay available.

I reinstalled the game and lost my coins. Coins and progress are stored on your device only, so they are not restored after you delete and reinstall the game. Remove ads is restored with Restore purchases in Settings.

The colors are hard to tell apart. Turn on Colorblind mode in Settings.

På svenska

Mynt och framsteg sparas bara på din enhet och återställs inte om du tar bort och installerar om spelet. "Ta bort reklam" återställs med "Återställ köp" under Inställningar i spelet. Har du svårt att skilja färgerna åt, slå på Färgblindläge under Inställningar.
