Twinswipe Privacy Policy

På svenska

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Twinswipe ("the app") is made by Ola Lindsten ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We do not run any server and do not receive your progress.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, within the choices you make in the consent and tracking prompts.
  • In-app purchases go through Apple or Google, and are managed by RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played (for example which levels players finish or give up on), within the choice you make in the consent prompt, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. Neither receives your name, email or advertising ID.
  • If you allow notifications, the app reminds you of your daily reward. The reminders are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, using the standard app storage of iOS or Android:

  • Campaign progress (stars per level), daily puzzle streak and endless progress.
  • Your coin balance, your daily reward streak, and how many rewarded ads you watched today.
  • The hour you usually open the app, used only to time the daily reward reminder.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, theme and the reminder switch.
  • A small cache of generated daily and endless puzzles.

This information is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your device backup (iCloud or Google), which is handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that give coins. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers: the Advertising ID on Android, and on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking (see below).
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped.
  • Diagnostic data about the SDK's performance.

Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices in the consent prompt and, on iOS, the tracking prompt. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use contextual information rather than your profile.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you buy "Remove ads", the app stops showing full-screen ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay available as an optional way to earn coins.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

Before any ad is loaded, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Your choice is stored on your device by the UMP SDK. Where it applies, you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy settings. If the app cannot check your consent status, for example because the consent service cannot be reached, it requests no ads until it can.

Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

On iOS, the app asks for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. This permission only affects whether Google can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads and measurement. The app works the same whether you allow it or not. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Notifications

The app can remind you once a day when the daily puzzle and your daily reward are waiting. It asks first in one sentence, after you claim your first daily reward or win your first daily puzzle, and shows the system's permission prompt only if you tap "Remind me"; turning on "Daily puzzle and reward reminder" in Settings asks too. It never asks when you first open the app. The reminders are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device, at the hour you usually open it, and no push service, server or device token is involved. You can turn them off in the app under Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve the game, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google: when a level is started, completed or left unfinished or restarted (with the level number, moves, time, stars, and the hints and undos used), tutorial steps, skips, chapters reached and completed, Expert level attempts and results, daily puzzle results and whether you shared one, new mechanics and features unlocked, which offers and level cards were shown and what you chose, when an ad was shown or a rewarded ad finished, which product or cosmetic was bought, the highest level you have reached, and how many days have passed since you installed the app. Firebase also records basic information such as the app version, device model, operating system, language and an approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region, not your precise location).

These events do not include your name, email address, advertising ID or any other information that identifies you. The app does not set a user ID. Firebase assigns a random app instance ID to tell sessions of the same installation apart.

Your choice in the consent prompt applies: if you do not consent to storing and accessing information on your device, analytics runs in Google's consent-denied mode, which stores no identifier on your device and sends only limited, cookieless measurements. Analytics data is never used for advertising. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy settings" in Settings, where it is offered. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google, receives a crash report: the error and where in the app it happened, the screen and level you were on, the app version, device model and operating system, and a random installation ID used to count how many installations a crash affects. Crash reports contain no personal information and are used only to find and fix bugs.

In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

The app offers "Remove ads" (a one-time purchase), a one-time starter pack (no ads between levels plus coins) and coin packs. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment card or billing details.

Purchases are managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which helps the app verify and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history for this app (products, prices, transaction identifiers and dates), and basic device information such as operating system, app version, country and language. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email address from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use push notifications or any server of our own.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising, and we do not link it to your identity.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact us at twinswipe.app@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google and RevenueCat keep the information they receive according to their own policies. Analytics events are kept for 2 months; crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. To ask RevenueCat to delete purchase data linked to your anonymous app user ID, contact us and we will forward the request.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Since we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled by the consent and tracking settings described above, or by contacting Google or RevenueCat directly. You can also contact us and we will help.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Sweden the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), elsewhere the authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
